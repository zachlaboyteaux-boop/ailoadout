## Reply guidelines
1. **Add value first.** Reply with a useful fact, a tip, or our honest take ("We rank X S-tier for Y because..."). No "great post!" filler.
2. **Be fast on launches.** For a big launch, reply within the first hour with what it changes for builders.
3. **Link sparingly.** At most 1 in 5 replies includes a link, and only when it directly answers the thread (e.g. our /compare/ page in a "X vs Y?" thread). Never link in a first reply to a stranger.
4. **No spam patterns.** No identical replies, no tagging people into threads, no mass-mentioning, no DMs asking for follows.
5. **Neutral "we rank" voice.** Light gamer flavor (tiers, loadouts, "equip"), never mocking a tool or its makers.
6. **Real data only.** Tiers and verdicts must match tools.json; don't invent benchmarks or pricing.
7. **Disclose.** If a tool is a paid Featured listing, say so when we mention it.
8. **Daily rhythm:** 10–15 thoughtful replies/day, 3 queued posts/day, quote-post one launch/day with our take.
9. **Skip drama.** No politics, no pile-ons, no arguing with trolls.
