How to use it

  1. Open your tool's page from its loadout.
  2. Scroll to "Badge for " and copy the snippet.
  3. Paste it in your site footer, README or docs.

Rules

  • Badges reflect our editorial tier; if your tier changes, update the snippet (the image URL is per tier).
  • Badges are free. A paid Featured listing does not change a tier.
  • Not listed yet? Submit your tool.
